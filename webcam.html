Webcam Hand Bench

Tracks your hands with the laptop camera (MediaPipe), converts them to the game's 25-joint hand data, and shows the features the shape classifier will use. Nothing leaves your computer. The Hand Lab has the same readout with mock hands, replays and a 3D view.

Camera off
Press Start camera and allow camera access.
The first start downloads the hand model (about 8 MB).

The picture is mirrored like a selfie, so your right hand appears on the right. Depth comes from a single camera and is approximate, and a webcam sees your hand from the front, not from above like a headset. Use this page to check shapes and features. Real tracking noise needs a headset. The shape label is a rough guide only; the real classifier adds palm direction, hold time and hysteresis.
